Arcam Radia Wiring

This is the experimental bench wiring that produced repeated replies on one A25. Terminal 4 exceeds the ESP32 input rating. Long-term reliability is unknown. With the adapter and any cable disconnected from both devices, establish which plug contacts reach which screw terminals before using the table. Stop if the mapping is uncertain.

Notes

  • The amplifier's jack is 6P4C: only the middle four contacts exist. They meet adapter terminals 2 to 5, so terminals 1 and 6 touch nothing.
  • The numbers here are the adapter's own printed numbers. Adapters differ, so check the numbers on yours before you wire it.
  • A photo of the genuine Arcam cable shows its wires reversed end to end. Count from the adapter, not from a cable.
  • Nobody has checked yet whether powering the Atom from a USB supply brings ground hum into the audio.
  • Never wire the red 5 V. Tape its end and leave it loose.

About the resistor

The bench used bare wire. While the amplifier answers, terminal 4 swings to about ±5.4 V. The ESP32 input range is −0.3 to 3.6 V. Keeping replies short limits exposure time.

The resistor view illustrates a proposed variation. A 10 kΩ series resistor gives an estimated negative clamp current of about 0.5 mA. The project has no bench measurements for the 4.7–22 kΩ range or qualification of a protected interface.
